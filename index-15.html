Frequently Asked Questions
Is it free to download YouTube thumbnails?
Yes, 100% free. There are no hidden charges, no premium plans, and no download limits. You can download as many thumbnails as you want, anytime.
What is the highest quality thumbnail available?
The highest quality is "Max Resolution HD" at 1280×720 pixels. However, not all videos have this size — it depends on the original uploader's settings. If Max HD isn't available, the next best quality will be shown first.
Can I download thumbnails from YouTube Shorts?
Yes! This tool supports YouTube Shorts links (youtube.com/shorts/…), regular video links, and live stream URLs.
Is it legal to download YouTube thumbnails?
Thumbnails are publicly accessible images hosted by YouTube's CDN. Downloading them for personal or research use is generally accepted. However, using someone else's thumbnail commercially or re-uploading it without permission may infringe copyright. Always respect the original creator's rights.
